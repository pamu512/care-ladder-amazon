Chatterbox VO settings (reproduce with scripts/generate_amazon_demo_vo_chatterbox.sh)

Engine: chatterbox-tts (Resemble AI), CPU, ChatterboxTTS.from_pretrained
References (10 to 20s, conversational, not news-reader, not fast):
  refs/narrator_ref.wav  edge-tts en-IN-PrabhatNeural rate -15% (prompt only)
  refs/alexa_ref.wav     edge-tts en-US-AvaNeural rate -5% (prompt only)
  refs/neighbor_ref.wav  edge-tts en-IN-NeerjaNeural rate -8% (prompt only)
Edge-tts clips are never mixed into the final tape.

Per-voice generate():
  Narrator  exaggeration=0.45  cfg_weight=0.35  temperature=0.8
  Emotional mum lines (see seeds.tsv emotion=1):
            exaggeration=0.60  cfg_weight=0.30  temperature=0.8
  Alexa     exaggeration=0.30  cfg_weight=0.50  temperature=0.60
  Neighbor  exaggeration=0.55  cfg_weight=0.40  temperature=0.85

Takes: 3 seeds per sentence on first run; chosen seed recorded in seeds.tsv.
Later runs use seeds.tsv only (LINE=b03 regenerates one clip).

Edit gaps (in beat assembly, not TTS):
  0.32 to 0.48s between sentences (varied by clip hash)
  0.90 to 1.15s around the two emotional mum sentences
Intra-beat subclip gaps (b07a/b07b, b08*, b10*, b11*) use the same table.

Mix (ffmpeg, same chain for TTS and docs/demo/vo/anoop/ overrides):
  highpass 80 Hz
  equalizer -2 dB at 300 Hz
  acompressor threshold=-18dB ratio=2.5 attack=12 release=120
  short room: aecho=0.8:0.88:36:0.18
  Alexa extra: highpass 200 Hz, lowpass 6 kHz, +1.8 dB at 2 kHz
  continuous room-tone bed at about -55 dB (never digital zero)
  loudness I=-16 LUFS, TP=-1.5, LRA=11
  no hard noise gate

Music: original soft piano-like bed (docs/demo/vo/chatterbox/music.wav),
no lyrics, generated for this tape (no third-party sample).
Ducked to about -22 dB under speech; silent on b01 (mum open);
back from b11 (resolution).

Anoop override: if docs/demo/vo/anoop/<clip>.wav exists, it replaces the
narrator TTS clip and runs through the same processing chain.
