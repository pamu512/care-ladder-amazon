Chatterbox VO settings (reproduce with scripts/generate_amazon_demo_vo_chatterbox.sh)

Engine: chatterbox-tts (Resemble AI), CPU, ChatterboxTTS.from_pretrained
References (~15 to 16s of real speech, conversational, length matters most):
  refs/narrator_ref.wav  edge-tts en-IN-PrabhatNeural (prompt only)
  refs/alexa_ref.wav     edge-tts en-US-AvaNeural (prompt only)
  refs/neighbor_ref.wav  edge-tts en-IN-NeerjaNeural (prompt only)
Edge-tts clips are never mixed into the final tape.

Per-voice generate() (V4 knobs from the sister OpenCV demo):
  Narrator  exaggeration=0.55  cfg_weight=0.38  temperature=0.80
  Emotional mum lines (see seeds.tsv emotion=1):
            exaggeration=0.62  cfg_weight=0.32  temperature=0.80
  Alexa     exaggeration=0.30  cfg_weight=0.50  temperature=0.60
            extra takes on "Are you okay?" (b08b)
  Neighbor  exaggeration=0.62  cfg_weight=0.38  temperature=0.82
            extra takes on "I'm going." / "She's okay."

Takes: 3 seeds per sentence (8 for b08b, 6 for b10b/b11a).
Pick: whisper tiny.en WER match + highest f0 std + no clipping + no mid-phrase pause.
Then a duration sanity check. Chosen seed recorded in seeds.tsv.
Never slow or speed with atempo.

Edit gaps (in beat assembly, not TTS):
  0.20 to 0.35s between sentences (room tone, never digital zero)
  ~0.45 to 0.50s at beat changes (crossfade, last frame held, no black tpad)
Soft breaths from the model are kept; edge clicks only are trimmed.

Mix (ffmpeg, same chain for TTS and docs/demo/vo/anoop/ overrides):
  highpass 80 Hz
  equalizer -2 dB at 300 Hz
  presence +1.4 dB at 3500 Hz
  acompressor threshold=-18dB ratio=2.5 attack=12 release=120
  short room: aecho=0.8:0.88:36:0.18
  Alexa extra: highpass 200 Hz, lowpass 6 kHz, +1.8 dB at 2 kHz
  continuous room-tone bed (never digital zero)
  loudness I=-16 LUFS, TP=-1.5, LRA=11
  no hard noise gate, no atempo

Music: original soft piano-like bed (docs/demo/vo/chatterbox/music.wav),
no lyrics, generated for this tape (no third-party sample).
Ducked to about -22 dB under speech; out on b01 (mum open);
back from b11 (resolution).

Anoop override: if docs/demo/vo/anoop/<clip>.wav exists, it replaces the
narrator TTS clip and runs through the same processing chain.
